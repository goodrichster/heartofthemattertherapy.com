How I Work

Direct, engaged, and relational. We slow down enough to see what is happening beneath the surface so change is not temporary. It becomes structural.

Who This Is For

Thoughtful adults and couples who value insight, honesty, and therapy that goes beyond coping strategies toward deeper change.

This may be a fit if...

You feel stuck in relational patterns you do not fully understand, notice shutdown or reactivity in close relationships, and want to feel more grounded and less internally conflicted.

Meaningful change starts with seeing the deeper pattern clearly.

In our work together, we look carefully at what is happening beneath the surface. As those patterns become clearer, new choices and new ways of relating become possible.

Sometimes the work centers on a relationship. Sometimes it centers on your internal world. Often it includes both.

Thoughtful, capable, and motivated, but still stuck.
