How I work.

My style is direct, engaged, and relational. I pay close attention to what happens in the moment, both in what you describe and in what unfolds between us. Patterns often reveal themselves there.

This is active, focused therapy, not passive listening and not rigid protocol. Together we work to understand the emotional and relational patterns actually driving the problem rather than only managing symptoms.

As those patterns become clearer, new choices and new ways of relating become possible.

This work may be a fit if...

  • You feel stuck in relational patterns you do not fully understand
  • You notice shutdown, overfunctioning, or reactivity in close relationships
  • You want to feel more grounded and less internally conflicted
  • You value insight and are open to being thoughtfully challenged
  • You want therapy that moves beyond coping strategies toward deeper change

Thoughtful, capable, and motivated, but still feeling stuck.

I work with adults and couples who are insightful and responsible but caught in patterns that do not respond to surface-level fixes. Therapy with me is collaborative. We move at a pace that allows for depth without getting lost.

Training includes a Master’s in Social Work from Boston University, work at MIT’s Office of Violence & Prevention Response and the Albert & Jessie Danielsen Institute, and advanced training in Relational Life Therapy.

FAQ

Practical details, clearly answered.

Questions about working together often come down to logistics, fit, and what therapy will actually feel like. These answers keep that part straightforward.

Many people start with an initial consultation. If it feels like a fit, we schedule a 60-minute intake session and begin from there.

You do not need to be in crisis. If something feels stuck, overwhelming, or worth understanding more deeply, therapy can help.

Length depends on your goals. Some people want focused support for a specific issue, while others stay longer as patterns and relationships unfold over time.

Sessions are virtual only and take place through a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform.

This is an out-of-network practice. Clients pay at the time of service and can submit a superbill for possible reimbursement.
