About

Therapy that is thoughtful, direct, and responsive.

I work with adults and couples who are capable, motivated, and often successful on the outside, but still feel caught in patterns that do not respond to surface-level fixes.

The work is collaborative. Sometimes we slow down. Sometimes we accelerate clarity. The pace adapts to what is happening in the room so that insight leads to real movement.

Whether the focus is individual therapy or couples work, the lens stays relational. Early experiences, emotional responses, and attachment patterns shape the present in ways that often remain invisible until we look closely together.

Approach

Depth with responsiveness.

Therapy with me is active and collaborative. We work to understand the emotional and relational patterns actually driving the problem rather than only managing symptoms.

We move at a pace that allows for depth without getting lost, with enough structure to keep the work useful and grounded.

Training

Grounded and continually deepening.

Training includes a Master’s in Social Work from Boston University, work at MIT’s Office of Violence & Prevention Response and the Albert & Jessie Danielsen Institute, and advanced training in Relational Life Therapy.

The work is informed by relational, attachment-based thinking and shaped by a strong respect for emotional honesty, accountability, and real change.

Relational Lens

Insight that leads to movement.

The goal is not just insight for its own sake. The goal is to understand the deeper pattern clearly enough that meaningful change becomes possible in the places that matter most.

That can mean slowing down enough to feel what is happening, or working more actively and directly when the pattern is already visible and ready to shift.

Clients Often Appreciate That I...
  • Get to the heart of the matter
  • Do not shy away from what is uncomfortable
  • Help people see patterns quickly
  • Facilitate honest, productive conversations

This is not passive listening and not rigid protocol. Together we look at the emotional and relational patterns actually driving the problem, whether that means examining a moment carefully or moving quickly when clarity arrives.

What clients can expect

  • Attuned attention to what happens in the moment
  • Thoughtful challenge when avoidance or old roles take over
  • Work that goes beyond coping strategies toward deeper change
  • A pace that balances depth, honesty, and momentum

This may be a fit if you...

  • Feel stuck in relational patterns you do not fully understand
  • Notice shutdown, overfunctioning, or reactivity in close relationships
  • Want to feel more grounded and less internally conflicted
  • Value insight and are open to being thoughtfully challenged
