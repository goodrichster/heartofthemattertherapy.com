How I work.

My style is direct, engaged, and relational. I pay close attention to what happens in the moment, both in what you describe and in what unfolds between us. Patterns often reveal themselves there.

This is active, focused therapy, not passive listening and not rigid protocol. Together we work to understand the emotional and relational patterns actually driving the problem rather than only managing symptoms.

As those patterns become clearer, new choices and new ways of relating become possible.

This work may be a fit if...

  • You feel stuck in relational patterns you do not fully understand
  • You notice shutdown, overfunctioning, or reactivity in close relationships
  • You want to feel more grounded and less internally conflicted
  • You value insight and are open to being thoughtfully challenged
  • You want therapy that moves beyond coping strategies toward deeper change

Thoughtful, capable, and motivated, but still feeling stuck.

Frequently asked questions.

Practical details matter. Here are answers to some of the questions people often have when they are considering working together.

What can I expect during my first appointment?

Many people begin with a free 15-minute consultation. If the fit feels right, we schedule a 60-minute intake session focused on what is bringing you in and what you hope will change.

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

You do not need to be in crisis. If something feels stuck, overwhelming, or worth understanding more deeply, therapy can help. A consultation call can be a useful way to talk through whether it feels like the right next step.

How long will I need to be in therapy?

Length depends on your goals. Some people want focused help with a specific issue, while others choose longer-term work as patterns, relationships, and personal growth unfold over time.

Is individual therapy required before couples therapy?

No. In this approach, much of the individual work can happen in the context of the relationship itself, where the pattern actually shows up. That often deepens empathy and supports change more efficiently.

When are appointments available?

Telehealth appointments are available Monday through Friday between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

Do you offer in-person or online sessions?

Sessions are virtual only and take place through a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform designed to protect your privacy while fitting more easily into a busy life.

Do you accept insurance?

This is an out-of-network practice. Clients pay at the time of service and can submit a superbill for possible reimbursement if their plan includes out-of-network benefits.

What are your fees?

Individual 45-minute sessions are $200. Couples 45-minute sessions are $250. Couples 80-minute sessions are $440. A limited number of reduced-fee slots may be available.

What is your cancellation policy?

I ask for at least 48 hours’ notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Appointments canceled with less than 48 hours’ notice, as well as missed sessions, are charged the full session fee.
