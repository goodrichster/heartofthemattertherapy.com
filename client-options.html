Therapy Website Concepts

Design directions for client review.

This presentation organizes five homepage directions for the therapist’s content. Each concept aims at upscale, emotionally intelligent clients, but varies in warmth, artistic expression, and how strongly the therapist’s personality leads the brand.

Option A

Editorial Calm

The current direction: light, open, refined, and premium. This option feels grounded and modern, with restrained warmth and a strong emphasis on clarity and trust.
