Individual Therapy

When insight isn’t enough.

You may already have a lot of insight into what’s not working, yet still find yourself stuck in the same patterns. Individual therapy is grounded in a relational approach, even when the focus is not on a specific relationship.

We look at how earlier experiences shaped your patterns, and how they show up now. Over time, this creates more clarity, flexibility, and choice in how you respond.

Common areas of focus

  • High-functioning anxiety
  • Emotional reactivity or shutdown
  • Burnout and internal pressure
  • Repeated patterns in dating or partnership
  • Trauma held beneath competence
  • Difficulty expressing needs or setting boundaries
The Work

Relational, active, and focused on lasting change.

We look closely at how these patterns show up in your life now and how they developed over time. The work is active, engaged, and focused on helping you create meaningful, lasting change.
