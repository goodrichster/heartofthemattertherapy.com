Individual Therapy

Insight-oriented therapy for the patterns beneath competence.

Individual therapy is grounded in a relational approach, even when the focus is not a specific relationship. We look at how early experiences shaped your nervous system, expectations, and automatic ways of protecting yourself.

The goal is to understand how those patterns show up now, so they no longer quietly organize your life from the background.

Common areas of focus

  • High-functioning anxiety
  • Emotional reactivity or shutdown
  • Burnout and internal pressure
  • Repeated patterns in dating or partnership
  • Trauma held beneath competence
  • Difficulty expressing needs or setting boundaries
The Work

Active, engaged, and emotionally precise.

Sessions are not generic check-ins. We examine the moments, reactions, and internal conflicts that carry the most information, then use them to understand the deeper pattern underneath.

That depth makes the work particularly well suited for clients who are insightful but frustrated that insight alone has not translated into change.
