Interrupt the cycle and rebuild connection.

Couples therapy focuses on identifying and interrupting the negative cycles that keep you stuck while strengthening emotional safety, responsiveness, and connection.

We slow down reactive moments to see what is actually happening beneath the surface and begin building new ways of relating from there.

You may notice...

  • The same argument repeating over and over
  • One partner reaching out while the other pulls away
  • Escalation that leaves both of you misunderstood or alone
  • A quiet distance that has slowly grown over time
