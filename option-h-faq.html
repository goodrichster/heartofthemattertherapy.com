Frequently asked questions.

Practical details matter. Here are answers to some of the things people often wonder about when considering therapy.

What can I expect during my first appointment?

Many people start with a free 15-minute consultation. If it feels like a fit, we schedule a 60-minute intake session and begin from there.

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

You do not need to be in crisis. If something feels stuck, overwhelming, or worth understanding more deeply, therapy can help.

How long will I need to be in therapy?

Length depends on your goals. Some people want focused support for a specific issue, while others stay longer as patterns and relationships unfold over time.

Do you offer in-person or online sessions?

Sessions are virtual only and take place through a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform.

Do you accept insurance?

This is an out-of-network practice. Clients pay at the time of service and can submit a superbill for possible reimbursement.

What are your fees?

Individual 45-minute sessions are $200. Couples 45-minute sessions are $250. Couples 80-minute sessions are $440.
