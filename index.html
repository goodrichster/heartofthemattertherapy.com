Therapy Website Concepts

Design directions for client review.

This presentation organizes eight homepage directions for the therapist’s content. Each concept aims at upscale, emotionally intelligent clients, but varies in warmth, artistic expression, and how strongly the therapist’s personality leads the brand.

Safe

Most aligned with the current brief.

These are the strongest low-risk directions if polished, upscale, and trustworthy is the priority without pushing too far into experimentation.

Option A

Editorial Calm

The current direction: light, open, refined, and premium. This option feels grounded and modern, with restrained warmth and a strong emphasis on clarity and trust.

Bold

Useful for pushing the conversation.
