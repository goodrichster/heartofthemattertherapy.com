Therapy Website Concepts

Focused design directions for review.

This shortlist narrows the presentation to four viable directions. Each one supports an upscale, emotionally intelligent therapy practice, but varies in structure, atmosphere, and how immersive the page experience feels.

Shortlist

Four concepts worth comparing directly.

Option A is the clearest baseline. Option F pushes into immersion. Option H balances atmosphere with a more structured page. Option J takes F’s visual language and combines it with H’s clearer nav and a stronger FAQ presentation.
