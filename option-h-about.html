Thoughtful, capable, and motivated, but still stuck.

I work with adults and couples who are thoughtful, capable, and motivated, but stuck in patterns that do not respond to surface-level fixes.

My style is engaged and direct, balanced with responsiveness and care. Therapy with me is collaborative. We move at a pace that allows for depth without getting lost.

Clients often appreciate that I...

  • Get to the heart of the matter
  • Do not shy away from what is uncomfortable
  • Help them see patterns quickly
  • Facilitate honest and productive conversations

Relational at the core.

Whether I am working with individuals or couples, we focus on how early experiences, emotional responses, and attachment patterns shape present-day experiences and relationships, because when we get to the heart of the matter, change becomes possible.

Qualifications & training.

I have a Master’s in Social Work from Boston University and trained at MIT’s Office of Violence & Prevention Response and the Albert & Jessie Danielsen Institute. In 2022, I received a Social Work Grant Award from the Boston Psychoanalytic Society & Institute.

I am currently completing advanced training in Relational Life Therapy, an approach that integrates directness, accountability, and relational skill-building.
