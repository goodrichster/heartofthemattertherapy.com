Couples Therapy

Interrupt the cycle and rebuild emotional connection.

Couples therapy focuses on identifying the negative patterns that keep you stuck while strengthening emotional safety, responsiveness, and connection. The aim is not to replay the argument. It is to understand what fuels it.

By slowing down reactive moments and looking beneath the surface, we make space for the deeper longings, fears, and attachment needs driving conflict.

You may notice...

  • The same argument repeating over and over
  • One partner reaching while the other pulls away
  • Escalation that leaves both of you misunderstood
  • A quiet distance that has grown over time
Approach

See the deeper pattern together.

In this work, individual growth and relationship growth are not treated as separate projects when they can be addressed in the room together. That allows each partner to understand the other’s inner experience as it unfolds.

From there, therapy becomes a place to practice more honest, skillful, and connected ways of relating in real time.
