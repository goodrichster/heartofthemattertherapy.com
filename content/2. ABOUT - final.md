# **🌿 ABOUT**

I work with adults and couples who are thoughtful, capable, and motivated — but stuck in patterns that don’t respond to surface-level fixes.

My style is engaged and direct, balanced with responsiveness and care. Clients often tell me they appreciate that I:

* Get to the heart of the matter

* Don’t shy away from what’s uncomfortable

* Help them see patterns quickly

* Facilitate conversations that feel honest and productive

Therapy with me is collaborative. We move at a pace that allows for depth without getting lost. Sometimes we slow down. Sometimes we accelerate clarity. The work adapts to what’s happening in the room.

At its core, my approach is relational. Whether I’m working with individuals or couples, we focus on how early experiences, emotional responses, and attachment patterns shape present-day experiences and relationships—because when we get to the heart of the matter, change becomes possible.

**My Qualifications & Training**

I have a Master’s in Social Work from Boston University and trained at MIT’s Office of Violence & Prevention Response and the Albert & Jessie Danielsen Institute. In 2022, I was the recipient of a Social Work Grant Award from the Boston Psychoanalytic Society & Institute. I am currently completing advanced training in Relational Life Therapy (RLT), an approach that integrates directness, accountability, and relational skill-building. I bring years of life experience and a steady meditation practice to my work.

Most importantly, I am a lifelong learner. When something feels unclear—either in my work or in my own life—I take it as an invitation to look more closely. I read, reflect, and seek out perspectives that deepen and challenge how I practice. I believe that often, it’s the right question—not the right answer—that matters most.
