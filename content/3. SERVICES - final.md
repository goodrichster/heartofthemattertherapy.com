# **🌿 SERVICES \[dropdown menu\]**

## **Individual Therapy**

Individual therapy is grounded in a relational approach, even when the focus isn’t on a specific relationship.

Common areas of focus include:

* High-functioning anxiety

* Emotional reactivity or shutdown

* Burnout and internal pressure

* Repeated patterns in dating or partnership

* Trauma held beneath competence

* Difficulty expressing needs or setting boundaries

We look at how early relational experiences shaped your nervous system and patterns — and how those patterns show up now.

This work is active, engaged, and insight-oriented.

---

## **Couples Therapy**

Couples therapy focuses on identifying and interrupting the negative cycles that keep you stuck, while strengthening emotional safety, responsiveness, and connection. By getting to the heart of the matter, we work to reclaim intimacy. 

You may notice:

* You and your partner getting into the same arguments over and over again

* One partner reaching out while the other pulls away

* Escalation that leaves both of you feeling misunderstood or alone

* A quiet distance that has slowly grown over time

In our work together, we slow down reactive moments to see what’s actually happening. We look beneath the surface to the deeper longings, fears, and attachment needs that drive conflict. From there, we begin to build new ways of relating, so that each of you feels seen, understood, and connected.
