## **The Heart of the Matter Therapy**

### **Depth-Oriented Therapy for Adults & Couples in Massachusetts**

**For people who want meaningful change, not surface-level fixes.**

*Telehealth therapy for adults and couples throughout Massachusetts.*

You may be capable, thoughtful, and responsible.

And yet something in your life isn’t working the way you want it to.

Maybe it shows up in your closest relationships.  
Maybe it shows up as anxiety, overthinking, reactivity, or emotional shutdown.  
Maybe you feel disconnected — from your partner, from others, or from yourself.

Often these experiences aren’t random. They’re shaped by deeper emotional and attachment patterns that developed over time and now operate automatically.

In our work together, we slow down and look carefully at what’s happening beneath the surface. As those patterns become clearer, new choices and new ways of relating become possible.

I work with adults and couples who want to get to **the heart of the matter** — the deeper emotional patterns driving what feels stuck — so that change isn’t temporary. It’s structural.

---

### **How I Work**

My style is **direct, engaged, and relational**.

I pay close attention to what happens in the moment — both in what you describe and in what unfolds in the room between us. Patterns often reveal themselves there.

Sometimes we slow down and examine a moment carefully. Other times insight arrives and the work moves more quickly.

This is **active, focused therapy** — not passive listening and not rigid protocol. Together we work to understand the emotional and relational patterns that are actually driving the problem, rather than only managing symptoms.

Sometimes the work centers on a relationship.   
Sometimes it centers on your internal world.  
Often it includes both.

The goal is deeper understanding and meaningful change.

---

### **This work may be a fit if …**

* You feel stuck in relational patterns you don’t fully understand

* You notice shutdown, overfunctioning, or reactivity in close relationships

* You want to feel more grounded and less internally conflicted

* You value insight and are open to being thoughtfully challenged

* You want therapy that moves beyond coping strategies toward deeper change
