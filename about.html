About

Therapy that is thoughtful, direct, and responsive.

I work with adults and couples who are capable, motivated, and often successful on the outside, but still feel caught in patterns that do not respond to surface-level fixes.

The work is collaborative. Sometimes we slow down. Sometimes we accelerate clarity. The pace adapts to what is happening in the room so that insight leads to real movement.

Clients often appreciate that I...

  • Get to the heart of the matter
  • Do not shy away from what is uncomfortable
  • Help them see patterns quickly
  • Facilitate honest, productive conversations
Approach

Relational at the core.

Whether the focus is individual therapy or couples work, the lens stays relational. Early experiences, emotional responses, and attachment patterns shape the present in ways that are often invisible until we look closely together.

The goal is not just insight for its own sake. The goal is to understand the deeper pattern clearly enough that meaningful change becomes possible.

Training

Grounded and continually deepening.

Training includes a Master’s in Social Work from Boston University, clinical work at MIT’s Office of Violence & Prevention Response and the Albert & Jessie Danielsen Institute, and a 2022 Social Work Grant Award from the Boston Psychoanalytic Society & Institute.

Advanced training in Relational Life Therapy informs an approach that blends directness, accountability, and relational skill-building.
