Frequently Asked Questions

Practical details, clearly answered.

Questions about working together often come down to logistics, fit, and what therapy will actually feel like. This version keeps that part clear while feeling more designed and spacious.

What to expect from the process.

Therapy often begins with a brief consultation, then moves into a fuller intake session if it feels like the right fit. From there, the work is tailored to your goals, your pace, and the patterns that most need attention.

A good fit often means
  • You want depth, not just symptom management
  • You are ready to look at patterns with honesty and care
  • You want a thoughtful, structured therapeutic relationship
01

What can I expect during my first appointment?

Many people begin with a free 15-minute consultation. If the fit feels right, the first full appointment is a 60-minute intake session focused on what is bringing you in and what you hope will change.

02

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

You do not need to be in crisis. If something feels stuck, overwhelming, or worth understanding more deeply, therapy can help. A consultation call is a useful way to talk through whether it feels like the right next step.

03

How long will I need to be in therapy?

Length depends on your goals. Some clients want focused support around a specific issue, while others choose longer-term work as patterns and relationships unfold over time.

04

Do you offer in-person or online sessions?

Sessions are virtual only and take place through a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. The process is designed to be private, streamlined, and easy to fit into a demanding schedule.

05

Do you accept insurance?

This is an out-of-network practice. Clients pay at the time of service and can submit a superbill for possible reimbursement if their plan includes out-of-network benefits.

06

What are your fees?

Individual 45-minute sessions are $200. Couples 45-minute sessions are $250. Couples 80-minute sessions are $440. A limited number of reduced-fee slots may be available.
